# Product

## Register

brand

## Users
Local families and individuals in the community seeking professional, reliable, and high-quality dental care. They value trust, hygiene, and a welcoming atmosphere where they feel cared for, not just like another patient.

## Product Purpose
A high-conversion landing page for "Odontología Integral" that establishes clinical authority and emotional trust. The goal is to move visitors from curiosity to action—primarily booking an appointment via WhatsApp—by showcasing expertise, modern facilities, and a patient-centric approach.

## Brand Personality
**Professional, Trustworthy, and Warm.**
The brand balances the clinical precision of a modern medical practice (Professional/Trustworthy) with the approachability of a family clinic (Warm/Welcoming). It should feel like an expert you can trust with your health, but also someone you're comfortable talking to.

## Anti-references
- **Corporate Sterile**: Avoid looking like a cold hospital or a generic insurance portal.
- **SaaS Monoculture**: No "AI-cream" backgrounds, generic rounded-pill buttons, or the "hero-metric" layout.
- **Stocky**: Avoid overly polished, fake-smiling stock photos; prioritize authentic clinical imagery.

## Design Principles
- **Professionalism with Heart**: Combine clean, clinical precision (grids, typography) with warm, human elements (authentic photos, soft spacing).
- **Clarity over Clutter**: Information architecture that respects the user's cognitive load, leading them naturally toward the CTA.
- **Patient-First Experience**: Every interaction should feel effortless and accessible, mirroring the care received in the clinic.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
- **WCAG AA Compliance**: All text-to-background contrast ratios must meet ≥4.5:1.
- **Legibility**: Minimum base font size of 16px with relaxed leading for accessibility.
- **Inclusive Interaction**: Full keyboard navigation support and descriptive ARIA labels for all interactive elements.
